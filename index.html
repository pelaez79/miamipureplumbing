Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why does Miami-Dade tap water require a water softener or filtration system?

Miami-Dade tap water comes from the Biscayne Aquifer, a limestone formation that loads the water with calcium and magnesium — typically 12 to 15 grains per gallon (GPG) of hardness. That mineral load builds up as limescale inside pipes, fixtures, and water heaters, shortening equipment life and driving up energy costs. Hard water also leaves spots on glassware, dulls laundry, and irritates skin and hair. A water softener or filtration system removes those minerals before they can do damage, protecting your home and improving the water you drink, cook with, and bathe in every day.

Are municipal permits included in the installation cost?

Yes. For water heater packages, the published package price includes the required HVHZ (High-Velocity Hurricane Zone) Miami-Dade municipal permit, code-required components such as thermal expansion tanks, and inspection filing and coordination. Your assigned licensed contractor pulls the permit under their license and manages the inspection process, so your installation is fully documented and code-compliant.

Who performs the actual plumbing installation?

All installations are performed by independent, state-certified plumbing contractors holding Florida CFC or PMC licenses and carrying full liability insurance. Miami Pure Plumbing pre-screens every contractor in its network and coordinates your appointment — the contractor performs the work, pulls the permits, and stands behind the installation.

You get the same licensed, insured Florida plumbing contractors (CFC/PMC) — plus fixed, published pricing with no surprises, permit handling, and coordinated scheduling. Our service costs you nothing extra: the installing contractor pays us a marketing fee, so the package price you see is the price you pay.

How do payments work? Do I pay Miami Pure Plumbing?

No. You never pay us. Contracting, warranties, and billing happen directly between you and your assigned licensed plumbing contractor at the published package price. We coordinate everything at no additional cost to you.
