Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why does Miami-Dade tap water require a water softener or filtration system?

Water sourced from the Biscayne Aquifer in Miami-Dade County is naturally hard, averaging 12 to 15 grains per gallon (GPG) of calcium and magnesium. This hard water leads to heavy limescale buildup in pipes, damages water heater heating elements, and causes skin irritation and soap scum.

Are municipal permits included in the installation cost?

Yes. All water heater replacements coordinated through our network include mandatory Miami-Dade County High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) plumbing permits, expansion tanks, and inspection filing as required by local building codes.

Who performs the actual plumbing installation?

Installations are performed exclusively by independent, state-certified plumbing contractors (holding active CFC or PMC licenses in Florida) with full commercial liability insurance.
